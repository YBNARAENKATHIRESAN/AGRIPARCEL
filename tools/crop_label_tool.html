How to decide

Paddy: a flat rectangle with thin ridges around it. It may be flooded, bright green, or tan after harvest.

Banana: dark green, rough-looking, planted in rows, and green all year.

Other: coconut groves, houses, river, bare land. Label a few coconut spots on purpose, so the model learns they are not banana.

  • Tap the middle of a plot, away from its edges.
  • Not sure? Skip it. A wrong label hurts more than a missing one.
  • Spread your spots across both taluks. Do not cluster them in one corner.
  • This picture is one date. Fields change through the year, so label only the clear cases.
  • Tap a dot to delete it. Press Z (or Undo) to remove the last one.
  • The picture is only for you to look at. The final map is built from Sentinel data.
